About

I'm a Computer Systems Engineer from TecNM Campus Orizaba who went on to complete a Master's degree in Cybersecurity at Universidad Internacional de La Rioja (UNIR) in Spain. My path has run through security analysis, IT consulting and now enterprise technical support at Microsoft.

In parallel I continue as an Associate Security Analyst at Fortra, where triaging detections, hunting threats through OSINT and owning high-touch client relationships has taught me to stay calm under pressure and to be precise when precision matters most. That discipline feeds directly into the support engineering work I do at Microsoft.

Outside the technical side, I've spent three summers as a camp counselor and adventure coordinator in the United States — which is where the communication, leadership and bilingual comfort came from. It turns out explaining a root cause analysis and explaining a ropes course have more in common than you'd expect.
